\section*{1 · Install}
\textbf{Windows:} download \texttt{wtWin-….exe} from \href{https://wtwin.bgg-home.de}{wtwin.bgg-home.de} and run it. The installer asks for the language first, needs no admin rights and replaces an older version by itself. On first launch choose “More info” › “Run anyway” (not signed).

\textbf{Linux:} \texttt{sudo apt install ./wttux\_…\_amd64.deb}

\textbf{macOS:} \texttt{…-arm64.dmg} (Apple chip) or \texttt{…-x64.dmg} (Intel); on first launch choose “Open Anyway” under System Settings › Privacy.
\section*{2 · Connect or work on your own}
\textbf{With your webtrees:} sign in to webtrees in the browser, menu \menue{App} › \menue{Connect with wtWin}. The program takes over address and account, asks once and opens the tree. Requires webtrees 2.2 with the api4webtrees module (included in nas4webtrees).

\textbf{By hand:} enter the address of your webtrees site, \menue{Connect}, sign in with user name and password. The same rights apply as on the website.

\textbf{Without a server:} on the right of the start screen \menue{Create family tree} or \menue{Import GEDCOM file …}. Afterwards you work without server, password or internet; webtrees runs invisibly in the background.
\section*{3 · Three layouts}
\textbf{Navigator} (\taste{Ctrl+1}): children on the left, the person with marriages in the middle, four generations of ancestors on the right. One click makes someone else the centre.

\textbf{Tree in the centre} (\taste{Ctrl+2}): person list, tree and person panel side by side; full screen for working in the tree.

\textbf{Family view} (\taste{Ctrl+3}): ancestors of both partners at the top, the couple in the middle, children with partners and grandchildren below.
\section*{4 · Create and check}
\textbf{Charts} (15 kinds, also plotter and large print), \textbf{lists} and \textbf{books} (ancestors, descendants, family book, also as a website) in the \menue{Create} menu, as PDF or print.

\textbf{Tools:} plausibility check with 61 rules, merge persons, clean up places, date calculator, church year.

\textbf{Sources, places, archive, tasks:} their own windows under \menue{View} (\taste{Ctrl+5} to \taste{Ctrl+9}).
\section*{5 · Help}
\taste{F1} opens the help for the current window. \taste{Ctrl+F} finds a person, \taste{F5} reloads, \taste{Ctrl+C} copies the person text.
