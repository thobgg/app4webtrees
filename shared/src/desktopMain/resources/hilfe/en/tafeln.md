# Charts

Open the **Create chart** window via **Create › Ancestor chart …** (Ctrl+Shift+T), **Create › Descendant chart …** (Ctrl+T) or the **Chart** icon. On the left you choose the chart type and the settings, on the right you see the preview. The preview is the finished sheet: what you see is what prints.

## Using the preview

![](desktop-tafel-fenster.jpg)


- **Mouse wheel** zooms around the pointer, **dragging** pans.
- **One click** switches between the whole sheet and reading size.
- **Right-click** on a box: colour or hide that person's branch.
- For large charts the window shows the number of persons and the sheet size in centimetres.

**Ancestors**

- **Ancestor chart:** central person at the bottom, one row per generation above, Kekulé numbers.
- **Ancestor chart (paged):** four generations per landscape A4 page, "→ p. 5" for the continuation, for filing.
- **Fan chart** (half circle) and **Ancestor circle** (full circle): the central person in the middle, one ring per generation.
- **Timeline:** ancestors as life bars over a year axis, who lived when; optionally with historical events.
- **Paternal line**, **Maternal line**, **Oldest ancestor** (line to the earliest known birth).

**Descendants**

- **Descendant chart:** all descendants on one sheet, the central person on top.
- **Descendant chart (paged):** few generations per page, references to the continuation, a page overview in front, the index of persons at the back.
- **Descendants of the grandparents:** uncles, aunts and cousins on both sides; father and mother side by side in the middle.

**Ancestors and descendants**

- **Hourglass:** ancestors above, descendants below the central person.
- **Hourglass of a couple:** the ancestors of both partners, their common descendants below; with several marriages you choose the partner.
- **Kinship chart:** the descendants of all ancestral couples of one generation (parents, grandparents, great-grandparents …) side by side, i.e. all blood relatives; duplicates get a grid reference.
- **Relationship path:** how two persons are related. Search the second person in the field below. The common ancestor on top, both lines below, the relationship in words (up to four paths).

## Settings

The settings are in groups, each can be expanded and collapsed. What you set is kept for the next chart.

- **Content:** central person, generations (for the hourglass separately for ancestors and descendants), siblings (none, of the central person, of all ancestors), spouses, both parents, name bearers only, ancestral couples. Filters: only married or with children, hide early deaths, hide branches (right-click). **Spouses** (family tree chart): no, as a line in the box, or as a separate box beside the person with a marriage line and the children below; plus “Siblings of the root person” in the top row and “Mark childless marriages”. With more than two marriages the line is used.
- **Box content:** date (year only, short, long), place (full, town only, town and district), baptism and burial as substitutes for missing birth and death, age at death, occupation, call name underlined, pictures, living persons with names only, hint where the chart ends ("+3 children"). Numbers: Kekulé (ancestors), chronicle (IV-12) or d'Aboville (1.2.3).
- **Design:** style (parchment, classic, colour, black and white), box shape (square, rounded, oval with round photo, shield), shadow, photo left of the box, background (white, parchment, paper texture, gradient, own picture), decorative frame (double line, ornamental corners, vines), curved lines, alignment, box width.
- **Large charts legible:** horizontal (generations as columns), central person on top or right, grid at the margin, index of persons on its own page, connect duplicates with a line.
- **Colours:** colour by sex, grandparent line or branch. **Colour rules** colour boxes by surname, given name, birth place, death place, occupation, sex or "living"; the first matching rule applies. The rules apply to all charts of this tree. Coloured branches (right-click) can be removed one by one or all at once.
- **Title and legend:** heading, line below the title, "compiled by", legend on the chart (symbols, numbers, lines, rules in words).
- **Large print and plotter:** see below.
- **Templates:** save all settings as a file (`.wtvorlage`) and load them again, e.g. for the family reunion or a second person.
- **Attach index cards:** one page per person behind the chart with dates, parents, marriages and children, notes and sources. In the PDF, a click on the box jumps to the card and back.

## Output

![](tafel-karteikarte.jpg)
![](tafel-ahnen-seiten.jpg)


- **Print on one sheet:** shrinks the chart to the printer's paper.
- **Save as PDF (poster):** at original size, for the copy shop or for sharing.
- **Print sheets** / **Sheets as PDF:** the chart spread over A4 sheets, with an assembly plan (see Large print).
- **Save as PDF for the plotter:** at roll width.
- **Save as image (PNG):** for a website or image editing.
- Paged charts have **Print** and **Save as PDF** for all pages.

## Large print

![](tafel-montageplan.jpg)


Under **Large print** you choose the size: original size, a **width in cm** or **On sheets** (columns × rows). The window shows the final size and the number of sheets, e.g. "final size 50 × 34 cm · 4 A4 sheets".

When printing sheets, the first page is the **assembly plan** with the labels A1, A2, B1, B2 … Each sheet overlaps its neighbour by 1 cm: trim at the marks and glue together.

For the **plotter** choose the roll width (91.4 cm or your own) and whether the chart is fitted to the roll width; if it is wider, several strips are produced. The PDF then has exactly the page size of the roll.
