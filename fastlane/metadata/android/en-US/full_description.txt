wtAnd is an Android app for webtrees, the free web-based genealogy software. It shows and edits the family tree that lives on your own webtrees server – no account with a provider, no subscription, no ads, nothing that holds your data hostage.

Features:

* The tree is the centre: hourglass view with ancestors, partners, children and grandchildren, plus siblings and cousins; pan and zoom freely, expand branches, make any person the focus. Zooming out, a card shows less rather than smaller, and the text stays readable
* Profile: life as a timeline (including marriage and the births of the children), relationship to yourself ("paternal grandfather"), photos, family, map of the stations of a life
* Godparents and witnesses below baptism and marriage; every person shows where they were a godparent or witness themselves
* Editing: add, change and delete events – also marriages and other family events; add relatives right in the tree with the "+" on every card; dates are picked, places are suggested from the tree
* Photos: take or choose a picture and attach it to a person; photo overview of the whole tree; viewer with swipe, pinch and double-tap
* Archive: if the server runs the Sammlungen module, the app also shows its archive – folder and thematic collections, and above all the pictures that hang on nobody. Capture a picture on the road with description, date and people, stored as EXIF inside the file
* Documents: PDFs open in the app's own viewer, page by page
* Messages: inbox from webtrees’ own messaging right on the start page – read, reply, delete, write. Share a person from the profile with fellow researchers; the link opens the profile for the recipient. wtAnd appears in other apps’ share menu and opens links of your own server
* Anniversaries: upcoming birthdays, wedding days and days of death, with an optional daily reminder that also reports new messages and pending approvals
* Tasks: view the tree’s research tasks, mark them done and add new ones from a profile
* Moderation: accept or reject pending changes in the app
* Compact on phones, comprehensive on tablets; German, English, French, Dutch and Spanish, switchable in the app

Whatever is not native (yet) opens as the webtrees page in the same session.

Requirements:

* A webtrees server (2.2.x) – your own or one you have an account on – with the module api4webtrees in modules_v4/ (https://github.com/thobgg/api4webtrees). The webtrees core stays untouched.
* Optional: the Sammlungen module (1.6 or newer) for the archive.

Privacy: the app signs in with your normal webtrees account; every request runs as that user, with the same privacy rules as on the website. Stored on the device: server address, user name and the session cookie – never the password. No cloud backup of the app data, no analytics, no ads, no Google services. The map loads its tiles from OpenStreetMap.

wtAnd is not an official webtrees product. The same code also runs on the PC as wtWin (Windows) and wtTux (Linux), see the project page.
